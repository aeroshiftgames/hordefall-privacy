Privacy Policy

Hordefall: Zombie Survivor (Android, package com.aeroshiftgames.zombiesurvivor)

Developer and data controller: AeroShift Games ("we")
Contact: aeroshiftgames@gmail.com
Effective date: 26 October 2026

1. Summary

  • We do not run servers for the game and do not receive your personal data.
  • Your progress is stored on your phone (and, if you allow it, in Android's backup to your Google account).
  • Ads are shown by Unity Ads, which collects device data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

2. What the game itself stores

Hordefall has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no friend lists and no analytics or crash reporting of its own. Your progress (currencies, levels, unlocked items, records, settings) and the receipts of your purchases are saved in the game's private storage on your device and are not sent to us.

Android's own app backup is enabled for the game: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may copy the game's save to your Google account so that your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. That copy is made and kept by Google under your Google account; we do not receive it. You can turn backup off, or delete backups, in your Android and Google account settings.

3. Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies, which acts as an independent controller of the data it collects. To show, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, Unity Ads may collect from your device: your advertising ID, device model, operating system and language, IP address (used to estimate an approximate location, not your precise location), app and ad interactions, and similar technical data. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any personalised ad, Unity Ads asks for your consent itself. You can decline; ads may then still appear, but without personalisation.
  • Changing your mind: during any ad, tap the Unity data-privacy icon to opt out, see or delete the data Unity holds. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings.
  • Your choice: rewarded ads (for example "watch an ad to revive") are always optional. Buying Remove Ads turns off the ads shown between runs.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not receive the data Unity collects; we receive only aggregate, non-identifying reports (for example how many ads were shown).

4. Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your name, e-mail or payment details. Google tells the game which product was bought and gives it a signed receipt; the game checks that signature on your device (to stop fake purchases) and keeps the receipt only there. Refunds are handled by Google Play.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state - to load ads and to process purchases.
  • Billing - to make purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID - used by Unity Ads as described above.
  • Vibrate - for vibration, which you can turn off in Settings.

Google's and Unity's libraries also add standard technical permissions (for example keeping the device awake while a task finishes, scheduling background work, and Android's privacy-preserving ad services for ad measurement). None of them gives access to your personal content. The game does not ask for your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Links that leave the game: The "RATE IT" button opens the game's Google Play page, and this policy and the terms open in your browser. Those pages are run by Google and GitHub under their own privacy policies.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK) and KVKK (Türkiye)

The game itself processes no personal data on our side. For ads, the legal basis is your consent for personalised advertising, and Unity's legitimate interest for showing non-personalised ads, measuring them and preventing fraud, as explained in Unity's policy. This data is collected automatically by the Unity Ads SDK while ads are requested and shown. For purchases, it is the performance of the contract between you and Google Play. The same applies to users in Türkiye under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK); where data is transferred abroad by Unity or Google, it is under their safeguards and, where required, your explicit consent.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu). Because we hold no personal data about you, requests about ad data are best made to Unity through the in-ad privacy icon or Unity's policy, and about purchases to Google. You can always write to us and we will help.

US residents (for example California): we do not sell personal information. Showing personalised ads through Unity may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some state laws; you can opt out at any time by deleting or resetting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalisation in your Android settings, or through the Unity privacy icon in any ad. We do not process sensitive personal information.

8. Retention and deleting your data

The game's data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game, which deletes it. A backup Android made of it lives in your Google account until you delete it there. Unity and Google keep their data for the periods stated in their own policies.

9. International transfers and security

Unity and Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies. The game sends nothing to us; communication with Unity and Google is encrypted by their SDKs.

10. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and contains cartoon violence and blood. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

11. Future features

If we add features that process personal data - for example cloud saves, Google Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, notifications, analytics, crash reporting or other ad networks - we will update this policy before those features are released and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent in the game.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: aeroshiftgames@gmail.com
